About Clear Slip

Clear Slip is a web application designed to help users create, track, manage, and analyze receipts efficiently. Our app is built using Django and Bootstrap to provide a seamless and responsive experience.

Whether you are a freelancer, small business owner, or anyone who wants to organize their financial records, Clear Slip ensures you can easily manage your receipts, review your sales dashboard, and keep track of pending transactions in a secure and user-friendly platform.

Our mission is to simplify financial tracking, save you time, and provide clear insights into your business or personal revenue.
