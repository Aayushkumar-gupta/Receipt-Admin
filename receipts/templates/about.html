About Receipt Admin

Receipt Admin gives people who sell without a shop a simple way to write up a sale, send the buyer a receipt and keep a record of every sale.

It is free to use and works on your phone, tablet or computer.

Why it exists

More and more people sell through Instagram messages, Facebook Marketplace listings and local meetups. There is no shop and no till, so when a buyer asks for a receipt or tax season comes around, there is often nothing to show apart from old chats and payment notifications.

Receipt Admin gives these sellers what a cash register gives a shop. It works the same way whether you sell shoes, clothing, home decor or a sofa you no longer need.
