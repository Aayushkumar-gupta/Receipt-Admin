About Receipt Admin

Receipt Admin is designed to help businesses create, manage and store receipts effortlessly. We help small business owners and independent operators record every sale in one secure and organized place.


Our goal is simple, to give you a clear and professional view of your business through Receipt Admin. From tracking receipts to reviewing sales trends using our Dashboard, Receipt Admin helps you stay organized and confident in every transaction.


At Receipt Admin, we believe financial clarity leads to smarter business decisions. Our mission is to simplify how you manage receipts, save valuable time, and help your business grow with confidence.
